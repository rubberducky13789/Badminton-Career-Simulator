How to Play & Game Rules

Every decision advances your career. Read the systems below before stepping onto the court.

🏸 Training system Choose technical or fitness training to earn small, gradual improvements. Development becomes harder at elite ratings, while excessive work drains stamina and increases injury risk.
🏆 Tournaments Easy, Medium, Hard, and Elite events unlock through visible OVR, title, skill, and ranking requirements. Draws become stronger each round and avoid repeatedly selecting recent opponents.
🌍 Rankings Successful tournament runs improve your world ranking. Points can decay between seasons, and active rivals may pass an inactive or struggling player.
📈 OVR (Overall Rating) OVR is a weighted blend of Technique, Speed & Footwork, Power, Defense, Physicality, and Mentality. Form and injuries add only small temporary modifiers.
🩹 Injuries system Low stamina, overtraining, and heavy match loads increase injury risk. Injuries interrupt training, weaken form, may reduce ability, and can potentially end a career.
🔥 Form and confidence Wins, rest, and positive events build momentum. Losses, inactivity, illness, burnout, and slumps reduce form and confidence, directly affecting match performance.
📅 Aging and career progression Choose a starting age from 14–35. Each action represents one week, and age increases every 52 weeks. Younger athletes generally develop faster; older athletes must manage fitness carefully to slow decline.
🎙 Retirement system The player retires at age 35, after financial ruin, or following a career-ending injury. A completed career receives a final record and achievement summary.
⚔️ Rivalries and storylines Repeated meetings, close matches, upsets, injuries, comebacks, title races, and ranking battles generate evolving rivalries, career chapters, and news stories.

Game guide

How to play

Build a complete athlete, manage the risks of a long season, and earn your place at the top of world badminton.

🏆 Your objective Reach world rank #1 and win the Season-Ending Championship before retiring at age 35.
📅 Weekly choices Every training, recovery, or tournament action uses one week. Your player ages after every 52 weeks.
📈 Ratings and overall Six badminton attributes create the sports-game OVR: Technique 24%, Speed 18%, Power 17%, Defense 16%, Physicality 15%, and Mentality 10%. Form and injuries apply small temporary adjustments.
💰 Player development Spend prize and sponsorship money to develop any of the six attributes. Higher ratings cost more and improve more slowly.
🏸 Tournament draws Players ranked outside the top 100 begin in qualification. Win each simulated match to advance through the Round of 64, Round of 32, Round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, and final.
🩹 Health and stamina Training and matches reduce stamina. Low stamina and overtraining increase injury risk; rest restores energy and helps injuries heal.
⛔ Career-ending conditions The career ends immediately after financial ruin, a career-ending injury, or reaching age 35 without completing the objective.
