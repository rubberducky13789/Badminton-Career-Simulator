How to Play & Game Rules

Every decision advances your career. Read the systems below before stepping onto the court.

🏸 Training systemChoose technical or fitness training to earn small, gradual improvements. Development becomes harder at elite ratings, while excessive work drains stamina and increases injury risk.
🏆 TournamentsPay entry fees and play each match from qualification or the Round of 64 through the final. Results affect prize money, development, form, confidence, rivalries, and ranking points.
🌍 RankingsSuccessful tournament runs improve your world ranking. Points can decay between seasons, and active rivals may pass an inactive or struggling player.
📈 OVR (Overall Rating)OVR summarizes technical skill, fitness, and confidence. It changes gradually through sustained development, strong performances, health, form, and age—not after every minor action.
🩹 Injuries systemLow stamina, overtraining, and heavy match loads increase injury risk. Injuries interrupt training, weaken form, may reduce ability, and can potentially end a career.
🔥 Form and confidenceWins, rest, and positive events build momentum. Losses, inactivity, illness, burnout, and slumps reduce form and confidence, directly affecting match performance.
📅 Aging and career progressionEach action represents one week, and age increases every 52 weeks. Younger athletes generally develop faster; older athletes must manage fitness carefully to slow decline.
🎙 Retirement systemThe player retires at age 35, after financial ruin, or following a career-ending injury. A completed career receives a final record and achievement summary.
⚔️ Rivalries and storylinesRepeated meetings, close matches, upsets, injuries, comebacks, title races, and ranking battles generate evolving rivalries, career chapters, and news stories.

Game guide

How to play

Build a complete athlete, manage the risks of a long season, and earn your place at the top of world badminton.

🏆 Your objectiveReach world rank #1 and win the Season-Ending Championship before retiring at age 35.
📅 Weekly choicesEvery training, recovery, or tournament action uses one week. Your player ages after every 52 weeks.
📈 Ratings and overallPermanent skill, fitness, and confidence create your NBA 2K-style overall. Stamina affects current match form without changing your career rating.
💰 Player developmentSpend prize and sponsorship money to permanently improve skill, fitness, or confidence. Entry fees and weekly expenses still apply.
🏸 Tournament drawsPlayers ranked outside the top 100 begin in qualification. Win each simulated match to advance through the Round of 64, Round of 32, Round of 16, quarter-finals, semi-finals, and final.
🩹 Health and staminaTraining and matches reduce stamina. Low stamina and overtraining increase injury risk; rest restores energy and helps injuries heal.
⛔ Career-ending conditionsThe career ends immediately after financial ruin, a career-ending injury, or reaching age 35 without completing the objective.
